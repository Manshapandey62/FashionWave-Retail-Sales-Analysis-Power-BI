
# FashionWave Retail Sales Analysis

📌 Project Overview

FashionWave Retail Sales Analysis is a Business Intelligence project focused on analyzing retail sales, profitability, product performance, store performance, weekend vs weekday sales, and the impact of discounting.

The analysis was developed using Power BI and a retail sales dataset to identify business trends and generate actionable insights for management.

🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profitability.
- Identify high- and low-performing product categories.
- Analyze product-level profitability.
- Compare weekend and weekday sales performance.
- Evaluate store and city performance.
- Understand the impact of discounts on profit margins.
- Develop data-driven recommendations for improving sales and profitability.

🔍 Key Insights

Category Performance

- Women is the leading category with approximately ₹13.10M in sales.
- Women also generates the highest category profit of approximately ₹4.17M.
- Kids has the lowest category sales at approximately ₹6.58M.

Product Performance

- Jeans is the highest-profit product with approximately ₹2.20M profit.
- Other high-profit products include Watches, Shoes, Ethnic Wear, and Dresses.

Weekend vs Weekday

- Weekday sales: ₹28.85M
- Weekend sales: ₹11.30M
- Weekend sales are approximately 0.39× weekday sales.
- This highlights an opportunity to improve weekend revenue.

Store & City Performance

- Store_15 has the highest sales at approximately ₹1.02M.
- Store_11 has the lowest sales at approximately ₹0.55M.
- Bhopal leads the city view with approximately ₹1.78M in sales.

Discount Impact

The analysis shows a relationship between higher discount levels and lower profit margins:

Discount Tier| Profit Margin
No Discount| 38.99%
1–15%| 32.17%
16–25%| 21.68%
26–30%| 13.90%

This suggests that targeted discounts should be preferred over broad, deep discounting.

💡 Recommendations

- Run targeted campaigns to improve weekend sales.
- Use high-performing categories and products such as Women and Jeans as promotional anchors.
- Review the performance of the Kids category.
- Focus local marketing efforts on lower-performing stores.
- Monitor store and category performance regularly using Power BI.
- Establish discount guardrails to protect profitability.
- Evaluate promotions using profit as well as sales.
